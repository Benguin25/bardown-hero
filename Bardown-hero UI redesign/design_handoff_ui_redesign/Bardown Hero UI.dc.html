WELCOME TO BARDOWN HERO
MAKE YOUR PLAY
Drag anywhere to draw from the puck. Lift to play. Time freezes while you aim.
Teal is your team. Red defenders race for passes and loose pucks. Lead a teammate into space or curve around pressure. A red pickup ends the rush.
Flick through the net to shoot. Move left or right for the side; follow through farther beyond the goal for a high shot. A centered low flick tests the pads for a rebound.
Tap a yellow powerup before drawing when one is available. Goals unlock levels. Earn all three stars together in one run.
